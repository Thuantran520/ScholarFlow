ScholarFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  •  Version 2.4.2
✓ 100% Local Processing — No Personal Data Collection ⏱ Compliant with Chrome Web Store & Mozilla Add-ons Policies
Privacy by Design statement: ScholarFlow is built on a strict Privacy by Design principle. All citation data, screenshots, recordings, redaction masks, cookie profiles, and settings are processed 100% locally on your own browser. The extension has no data-storage server, no analytics, no tracking, and never sells or shares user data with any third party.

1. What data does the extension process?

ScholarFlow only activates when you deliberately interact with a webpage. It processes:

  • Citation metadata: article title, author names, publication date, journal, DOI, and URL. Used only to format citations (IEEE, APA 7, Harvard, MLA 9, BibTeX, Vancouver, Chicago 17, ACS, AMA) and stored locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • Screenshots & recordings: captured frames and screen recordings are encoded in memory and saved locally to your device (PNG/WebM). Nothing is uploaded.
  • Redaction masks: blur/blackout/pixelate effects are applied directly to the page DOM in your browser. With v2.4.2, your masks are persisted per-site in chrome.storage.local so they survive a page reload — still fully local, never sent anywhere.
  • Redaction PIN: the optional PIN that locks the "View original" button is stored locally as a salted SHA-256 hash — never as plaintext, never transmitted.
  • Cookie Manager: reads and writes cookies for the current site only on your explicit request. Exported cookie files (JSON / raw string / Netscape cookies.txt) and cookie profiles are saved locally on your machine and never leave your device.

2. Browser permissions (Principle of Least Privilege)

  • activeTab — reads page metadata and captures the active tab only when you click the extension.
  • scripting — renders the capture selector and redaction overlays on your command.
  • storage — stores your saved bibliography, settings, redaction masks, and cookie profiles locally.
  • clipboardWrite — copies citations or screenshots to your clipboard.
  • sidePanel (Chrome/Edge) — opens the convenient side panel UI.
  • cookies — used only by the Cookie Manager, and only on your direct action. The extension never reads, logs, or shares cookies automatically.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — required so capture/redaction work on any page you visit; no data is collected from this broad access.
⚠️ Cookie security note: login cookies are sensitive. Only export and share cookie files with people or devices you fully trust. ScholarFlow is not responsible for consequences if a cookie file is exposed outside your control.

3. Third-party academic verification services

To verify a paper's identity and enrich metadata, the extension sends only public, anonymous GET requests to open international databases:

  • Crossref API & doi.org — DOI resolution and citation lookup.
  • OpenAlex API (api.openalex.org) — 250M+ open scholarly works.
  • arXiv API (export.arxiv.org) — math/CS preprint metadata.
  • YouTube oEmbed — video title and channel when citing educational videos.
All requests are public, anonymous, and never carry cookies, user IDs, or browsing history.

4. Your control & data deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

  • Right to erasure: delete individual entries or wipe the whole bibliography at any time.
  • Export: export saved references as .bib, .ris, .txt, EndNote .xml, or CSL .json.
  • Cookies & profiles: fully under your control — export, import, or delete at any time; nothing is synced automatically.
  • Redaction masks: clear all masks with one click ("Restore original page").

5. Open source & license

ScholarFlow is open-source software released under the MIT License. Anyone can inspect the full source code to confirm there is no tracking or malicious behavior.
