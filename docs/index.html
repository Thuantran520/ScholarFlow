What ScholarFlow does

Academic citation

Generate references in IEEE, APA 7, Harvard, MLA 9, BibTeX, Vancouver, Chicago 17, ACS, AMA — plus in-text guidance — and export to .bib, .ris, .txt, EndNote .xml, or CSL-JSON.

Privacy protection

Blur, blackout, pixelate, or hide sensitive content. Masks persist per site, support precise drag regions, and are protected by an optional PIN lock.

Smart capture

Capture full pages, tall tables, or selected elements — with redaction masks automatically applied to exported screenshots.

Sensitive-data auto-detect

Scan and mask emails, phone numbers, ID numbers, and card numbers in one click, or mask every occurrence of a keyword.

Cookie manager

View, edit, and delete individual cookies; export as JSON, raw string, or Netscape cookies.txt; and switch between saved profiles per site.

Research productivity

A Pomodoro focus timer, source verification, linked learning goals, notes, and a bibliography library keep your workflow organized.

How it works

1

Open a webpage

ScholarFlow reads the active page metadata and auto-detects the source type to help you build a clean academic profile.

2

Capture or redact

Select content to hide, mask sensitive data, capture a region, or take a full-page screenshot.

3

Export and reuse

Copy citations, save bibliography entries, and export references in the format your tools need.

Release workflow & privacy

The project supports packaging for Chrome/Edge and Firefox variants. The repo includes manifest files and packaging scripts so the extension can be generated for browser store submission.

ScholarFlow is 100% local — no servers, no tracking, no data collection. Read the full Privacy Policy to see exactly what each permission is used for.
